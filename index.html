4 — If the Church wants to test this properly

The 7-point design checklist distilled from the full literature review (what would make a Djedi-conducted experiment scientifically meaningful):

  • Preregister the hypothesis, sample size, and analysis before collecting a single photograph
  • Photograph ALL drops — no selection; publish every crystal or a pre-agreed random subset
  • Randomize dish positions in the freezer; temperature-log every position (the 2006 study's admitted gap)
  • Blind the photographer — codeword labels opened only after scoring
  • Objective metrics, not aesthetic panels: the symmetry/branch metrics in section 3, not human beauty ratings
  • Control for the freezing variables: same water source, same supercooling cycle, same examination temperature window
  • Correct for multiplicity: decide in advance which single comparison counts as "the result"

This is what separates a twentieth photograph album from a twenty-first experiment. The Church loses nothing by holding this standard — and gains a result the world would have to respect either way.
